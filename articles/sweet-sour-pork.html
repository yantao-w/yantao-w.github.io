Sweet and Sour Pork (糖醋里脊)

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read

Sweet and sour pork is a takeout favorite for good reason — crispy, tangy, and irresistibly glossy. Here's how to make the classic at home, better than delivery.

Ingredients

  • 400g pork loin or tenderloin, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 1 egg, 1/2 cup cornstarch (for coating)
  • Oil for frying
  • 3 tbsp ketchup, 2 tbsp sugar, 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, a splash of water
  • Diced bell pepper and pineapple chunks (optional)

The Crispy Coating

Coat the pork pieces in beaten egg, then toss in cornstarch until fully covered. This double-layer gives the signature crunchy crust. Shake off excess before frying.

Fry

Heat oil to medium-high. Fry the pork in small batches until golden and crisp, about 4-5 minutes. Drain on paper towels. For extra crunch, fry a second time quickly.

The Sauce

In the wok, combine ketchup, sugar, vinegar, soy sauce, and a little water. Simmer until it thickens and turns glossy. If using peppers and pineapple, stir-fry them in the sauce for a minute.

Finish

Return the crispy pork to the wok and toss until every piece is coated in the shiny sauce. Serve immediately over steamed rice.
