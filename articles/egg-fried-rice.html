Perfect Egg Fried Rice (蛋炒饭)

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read

Egg fried rice is the dish every Chinese family cooks when they want something fast, satisfying, and full of flavor. Here's how to make it taste like it came from a real wok — no restaurant tricks needed.

The Secret: Day-Old Rice

Cold, day-old rice is the single biggest secret to perfect fried rice. Fresh rice is too moist and turns sticky. If you only have fresh rice, spread it on a tray and let it dry for 15-20 minutes before cooking.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cold cooked rice
  • 2 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 2 tbsp oil (peanut or neutral)
  • 1/2 cup diced spring onions
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce (to taste)
  • Pinch of salt and white pepper

Method

  1. Heat a wok or large pan on high until smoking.
  2. Add oil, then pour in the beaten eggs. Scramble until just set, then push to one side.
  3. Add the rice. Break up clumps and stir-fry for 2-3 minutes.
  4. Add soy sauce around the edges, toss, and season with salt and white pepper.
  5. Stir in spring onions, toss once more, and serve hot.

Pro Tips

Keep the heat high, keep it moving, and don't overload the pan — that's what gives you the light, separate grains and the smoky "wok hei" flavor.
