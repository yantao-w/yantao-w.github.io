Chow Mein (炒面): Classic Stir-Fried Noodles

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read

Chow mein — stir-fried noodles with vegetables and a savory sauce — is a takeout classic that's easy to nail at home. Here's the recipe.

Ingredients

  • 300g fresh or dried chow mein noodles
  • 200g chicken, beef, or shrimp, sliced
  • Mixed vegetables: cabbage, carrot, bean sprouts, onion
  • Sauce: 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp oyster sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tsp sugar, splash of water
  • Garlic and ginger, chopped

Cook the Noodles

Cook the noodles until just tender (al dente), then drain and rinse briefly. Toss with a little oil so they don't stick. For extra texture, pan-fry the noodles until lightly crisp.

Stir-Fry the Protein

Heat a wok on high. Sear the sliced protein until just cooked through, then remove and set aside.

Bring It Together

In the same wok, stir-fry garlic, ginger, and the vegetables until crisp-tender. Add the noodles and sauce, and toss everything on high heat. Return the protein, toss once more, and serve immediately.

Pro Tip

Keep the heat high and keep tossing — the slight char gives chow mein its distinctive smoky flavor.
