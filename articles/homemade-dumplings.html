Homemade Dumplings (Jiaozi) From Scratch

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read

Dumplings are the heart of Chinese home cooking. Making them from scratch is a rewarding ritual — and easier than it looks. Here's the whole process, from dough to a golden, juicy dumpling.

Make the Dough

Mix 2 cups flour with about 3/4 cup warm water and a pinch of salt. Knead into a smooth ball, cover, and rest for 30 minutes. Resting makes the dough soft and easy to roll.

Make the Filling

  • 300g ground pork (or chicken)
  • 1 cup finely chopped chives or cabbage
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Salt, white pepper, a splash of water

Mix everything and stir well in one direction until the filling feels sticky and well combined.

Roll and Fill

Roll the dough into a log, cut into small pieces, and flatten each into a round wrapper. Place a spoonful of filling in the center, then pinch the edges to seal. Practice makes perfect — they don't need to be pretty.

Cook

Boil: drop dumplings into boiling water, stir, and cook until they float and are puffed (about 5-6 minutes). Pan-fry: fry in oil until golden, add a splash of water, cover, and steam until done for a crispy bottom.

Dipping Sauce

Mix soy sauce, black vinegar, and a drop of chili oil. Dip, eat, repeat.

Love the classics? Try egg fried rice and chicken corn soup to round out the meal.
