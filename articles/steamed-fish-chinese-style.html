Chinese Steamed Fish (清蒸鱼)

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read

Steamed fish is the centerpiece of many Chinese dinners — delicate, juicy, and clean-tasting. With this simple method, it's elegant and easy at home.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole fish (sea bass, snapper, or tilapia), scaled and cleaned
  • Ginger, sliced, plus a few julienned for garnish
  • Spring onions, julienned
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp light soy or fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp oil, a pinch of sugar

The Secret to Juicy Fish

Pat the fish dry and make a few shallow cuts on both sides. Place sliced ginger inside and on top — this infuses flavor and keeps the fish moist while steaming.

Steam

Bring water to a rolling boil. Steam the fish on a plate for about 8-10 minutes per 500g, or until the flesh flakes easily with a fork. Don't over-steam — it dries out fast.

Finish with Hot Oil

Drain any accumulated liquid, scatter julienned ginger and spring onions on top, then pour the soy mixture over. Heat oil until shimmering and pour it over the fish — this sizzles and releases the aromatics.

Serve

Serve the whole fish immediately with steamed rice. The clean, fresh flavor is the point — let the fish shine.
