Kung Pao Chicken (宫保鸡丁)

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read

Kung pao chicken is one of the most famous Chinese dishes in the world — and one of the easiest to make at home. Tender chicken, crunchy peanuts, and a glossy sweet-spicy sauce.

Ingredients

  • 400g chicken breast, cubed
  • 1/2 cup roasted peanuts
  • Dried red chilies and Sichuan peppercorns
  • Bell pepper and spring onion
  • Marinade: 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp cornstarch, a splash of oil
  • Sauce: 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp cornstarch, water

Marinate the Chicken

Toss the cubed chicken with soy sauce, cornstarch, and a little oil. This tenderizes the meat and helps the sauce cling. Rest for 15 minutes.

Stir-Fry

Heat a wok on high with oil. Fry the dried chilies and Sichuan peppercorns until fragrant, then add the chicken and stir-fry until just cooked and golden.

Finish

Add the bell pepper, pour in the sauce, and toss until it thickens and coats everything. Fold in the peanuts and spring onions at the last minute so they stay crunchy.

Pro Tip

Go easy on the Sichuan peppercorns if you're new to numbing heat — a little goes a long way. Adjust chilies to your spice level.

Love these flavors? Try mapo tofu or egg fried rice to round out the meal.
