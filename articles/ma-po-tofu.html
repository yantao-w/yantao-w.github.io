Mapo Tofu (麻婆豆腐)

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read

Mapo tofu is the ultimate Sichuan comfort food — silky tofu in a rich, spicy, numbing sauce. Here's the home version that captures all the flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 block firm tofu, cut into cubes
  • 100g minced pork (or omit for a vegetarian version)
  • 2 tbsp doubanjiang (fermented broad bean paste)
  • 1 tbsp Sichuan peppercorns, ground
  • Garlic, ginger, and spring onions, chopped
  • Chicken stock, soy sauce, a little sugar
  • 1 tsp cornstarch mixed with water

Method

  1. Blanch the tofu cubes in salted boiling water for 1-2 minutes — this firms them up and keeps them from breaking.
  2. Heat oil and fry the minced pork until browned and crispy.
  3. Add doubanjiang, garlic, and ginger. Stir-fry until the oil turns red and fragrant.
  4. Pour in stock, add the tofu gently, and season with soy sauce and a pinch of sugar. Simmer 5 minutes.
  5. Thicken with the cornstarch slurry, then stir in the ground Sichuan pepper.
  6. Garnish with spring onions and serve hot over rice.

The Sichuan Kick

Freshly ground Sichuan peppercorns add the signature numbing heat. Grind them just before finishing — the aroma fades fast.

Round out the meal with sweet and sour pork and egg fried rice.
