Classic Chinese Chicken Corn Soup

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read

Silky, sweet, and deeply comforting, chicken corn soup is a Chinese family favorite. It's also one of the easiest soups to make — one pot, simple ingredients, big reward.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups shredded cooked chicken
  • 1 can (or 1 cup) sweet corn, plus some crushed
  • 4 cups chicken stock
  • 2 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 3 tbsp water
  • Salt, white pepper, sesame oil, spring onion

Method

  1. Bring chicken stock to a simmer in a pot.
  2. Add the shredded chicken and sweet corn. Simmer gently for 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in the cornstarch mixture slowly until the soup thickens to a silky consistency.
  4. Turn the heat low and drizzle in the beaten eggs while stirring — they form delicate ribbons.
  5. Season with salt and white pepper, finish with a few drops of sesame oil and spring onions.

The Secret to Silkiness

The cornstarch slurry is what gives the soup its luxurious, silky body. Add it gradually and keep stirring for the smoothest result.

Serve it alongside egg fried rice and homemade dumplings for a perfect Chinese dinner.
