Tomato Egg Stir-Fry (番茄炒蛋)

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read

If there's one dish every Chinese family cooks, it's tomato egg stir-fry. Simple, fast, and deeply comforting — soft eggs, juicy tomatoes, and a savory-sweet sauce.

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 3 ripe tomatoes, cut into wedges
  • 1 tbsp oil, a little sugar
  • Salt, a splash of water
  • Spring onion for garnish

Scramble the Eggs

Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt. Heat oil in a wok on medium, pour in the eggs, and scramble until just set but still soft. Remove and set aside.

Cook the Tomatoes

In the same wok, add the tomato wedges and stir-fry until they soften and release their juices, about 3-4 minutes. Add a pinch of sugar to balance the acidity.

Combine

Return the eggs to the wok, add a splash of water, and gently toss everything together. Season with salt to taste. The eggs will soak up the tomato sauce — this is where the magic happens.

Serve

Garnish with spring onions and serve immediately over steamed rice. It's the perfect weeknight dinner.

Pro Tip

For a saucier version, cook the tomatoes a little longer and add an extra splash of water — the sauce is the best part.
