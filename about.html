About Wok & Wonton

Authentic Chinese food, made simple

Wok & Wonton is a free recipe site for anyone who wants to cook real Chinese food at home — no special tools or hard-to-find ingredients required.

What We Do

We publish clear, tested recipes for stir-fries, dumplings, noodles, soups, and more. Every recipe focuses on authentic flavor with steps that are easy to follow, whether you're a beginner or an experienced cook.

Our Philosophy

Real Chinese home cooking should be approachable. We believe great dishes come from good technique and fresh ingredients — not mystery equipment. If you can stir-fry, you can cook our recipes.

Why We Exist

We're here to bring the flavors of Chinese home kitchens to your table, one simple recipe at a time. Our content stays free and is supported by advertising.

Have a dish you'd like us to cover? Reach out via our Contact page.
